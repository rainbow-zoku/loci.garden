The palace

Your AI doesn't grow wiser. It stops starting over: every session opens with what the last one left.

Rooms

A named context space: Dev, Design, Research. Each room holds what happened there before.

The soul file

One file per room that your AI reads before it speaks: what the room is for, and what must never be forgotten there.

Crystals

A fact established once and never re-derived. Write it down and your AI stops asking.

The garden

Run it locally
  • 1

    Get the kit

    Clone the repo and copy templates/ into a folder of your own.

  • 2

    Point your AI at it

    Open the folder in any file-aware client (Claude Code, Cursor, Zed, a local model) and run FIRST-SESSION.md. It builds PALACE.md, the entry point.

  • 3

    Walk the palace

    Your AI reads the room before it answers. The palace grows the way you tend it.
