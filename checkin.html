How did your palace evolve?

loci ships as a baseline. It becomes a palace only once you live in it, and living in it changes it: a room you added, a ritual you bent, a rule you rewrote because ours did not fit how you think. We cannot see that drift from here, and it is the most useful thing you could show us. This is the standing channel between the loci we ship and the loci you actually run. It does not close. Come back whenever your palace has grown into something we did not predict.

  • +We ask for structure, not content. Directory shapes, question patterns, workflow descriptions. Nothing from inside your palace needs to leave it.
  • +Nothing leaves your machine until you email it yourself. This page has no backend and stores nothing. Your answers live in this browser only.
  • +Seven parts, 15 to 30 minutes. Answers autosave locally, so you can leave and come back.
  • +Every question is skippable. Partial is useful. Lapsed is data too.

When you finish, you compile your answers and send them yourself to themapisnory@tuta.io. Plain email is not end-to-end encrypted: your own provider keeps a copy. If that matters for your threat model, send from a throwaway address, or just reply in the chat. We keep submissions only until we have read them into the next round, then delete them. Ask anytime, with your codeword, to have yours removed sooner. We will not quote you publicly without asking you first.

First, the quick one.

How often does your palace actually get opened these days?

Part 1 of 7

Version snapshot

Find your local Loci/ folder (the setup files, next to your palace). Look for LOCI-CORE.md or PALACE-METHODOLOGY.md and note the version string. If neither exists, check your README. Read-only, structure only, run from your Loci/ folder:

show the one-liner (read-only, structure only)
grep -rhm1 "loci-core version" . --include="*.md" 2>/dev/null
git log --oneline -5 2>/dev/null

If you cloned the repo: paste your git log --oneline -5.

If you set up from templates: roughly when, and what the version string says.

Scan your palace and setup files. What have you modified or added that wasn't in the original? A list is fine. Directory dump is fine.

What's missing

What do you reach for that isn't there?

Part 3 of 7

What you changed (the part we read first)

What modifications did you make to the baseline templates or methodology?

What drove each change? (The use case, not just the mechanism.)

Part 4 of 7

Unexpected territory

Has loci started doing something you didn't plan for?

What does your palace's rhythm look like in practice? Daily ritual, reactive capture, weekly review?

Which crystal tier (◇/◈/◆) do you spend the most time in, and have you bent it to a different purpose?

Part 5 of 7

You and the AI

What do you run the palace with (Claude Code, Goose, local models, something else), and has that changed since you set up?

Does your assistant carry identity across sessions (a name, a voice, working habits it remembers), or does every session start cold?

Where do you not trust it? What does it get wrong about your palace often enough that you've built a habit around correcting it?

Part 6 of 7

Privacy and data

What's your threat model for your palace? Who specifically shouldn't be able to read it?

What did you decide to keep out for privacy reasons?

Have you modified anything because you didn't trust a particular data flow (sync, MCP, cloud AI)?

Part 7 of 7

The pitch

Two sentences: how do you explain what you built to a skeptical friend?

When did loci stop feeling like a tool and start feeling like a place? (If it hasn't, that's an answer too.)

What would need to exist before you'd recommend it to someone?

last step

Compile and send

Your answers are assembled into one markdown block below. Read it once, then copy it into an email to themapisnory@tuta.io. It never sends from this page.

  • Directory dump? Check it for personal or employer names in folder paths.
  • Part 6 (privacy) answers: share only what you are comfortable sitting in an inbox.
